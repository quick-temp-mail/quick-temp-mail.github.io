Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

🛡️ We operate this site with no backend server of our own. We do not collect, store or process any personal data on our end. Read below for full details.

Who We Are

TempMail is a free disposable email service. We provide a frontend interface that connects to third party email APIs to generate temporary email addresses. We do not operate our own mail server.

What Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. We have no backend server, no database, and no analytics. Specifically we do not collect:

  • Your name or identity
  • Your IP address
  • Your location
  • Your browsing behavior
  • Your email contents
  • Usage statistics

Data Stored On Your Device

Our site stores the following data locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device or reaches our servers because we have none.

  • Session Storage: Your temporary email address and session information. Automatically deleted when you close your browser tab. Used only to restore your inbox if you accidentally refresh the page.
  • Local Storage: Your theme preference (dark or light mode) only. Nothing else.
  • URL Hash: Your temporary email address may appear in your browser URL bar as a backup. This is visible in your browser history on your device only.

Third Party Email Providers

⚠️ Important: Emails sent to your temporary address are received and stored by third party services, NOT by us. We have no control over how they store or handle your emails.

We use the following third party APIs to generate temporary email addresses and receive emails:

  • Guerrilla Mail (guerrillamail.com) — Primary provider. Emails are stored on their servers temporarily. Subject to their own privacy policy at guerrillamail.com
  • 1secmail (1secmail.com) — Backup provider. Subject to their own privacy policy at 1secmail.com

We strongly recommend not using temporary email addresses for sensitive personal information as we cannot guarantee how third party providers handle your data.

Advertising

We display third party advertisements to keep this service free. Our ad provider may use cookies and tracking technologies on their ads. We do not control this. You may use an ad blocker if you prefer not to see ads, though we appreciate your support by whitelisting us.

Affiliate Links

Our site contains affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products we believe are relevant to our users.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies ourselves. Third party ad networks displayed on our site may set their own cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

GDPR — European Users

Since we collect no personal data ourselves our GDPR obligations are minimal. However third party providers we use may process data subject to GDPR. Please review their respective privacy policies. If you have GDPR related concerns contact us at the email below.

Children

This service is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Service Limitations

We cannot guarantee continuous availability of this service as it depends entirely on third party APIs. Email delivery, storage duration and availability are controlled by third party providers and may change without notice.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

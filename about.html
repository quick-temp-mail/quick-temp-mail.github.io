What Is TempMail

TempMail is a free disposable email service that lets you create temporary email addresses instantly with no registration required. Use it to protect your real inbox from spam, test websites or sign up for services you are not sure about.

Why We Built This

Every time you sign up for a website your real email gets added to mailing lists, sold to advertisers and flooded with spam. We built TempMail because everyone deserves the right to browse the internet without giving away their personal information.

How It Works

When you visit TempMail we generate a random email address for you using third party email APIs. Any emails sent to that address appear in your inbox automatically. The address expires after a set time and all emails are deleted.

Honest Disclaimer

We believe in transparency. Here is exactly how this works:

  • We do not own the email servers. Emails are received by Guerrilla Mail and 1secmail on their servers.
  • We are a frontend interface only. We display emails from their APIs in our cleaner interface.
  • We have no backend server of our own. Your session data stays on your device only.
  • Service availability depends on third party providers and may change without notice.

Privacy Commitment

We collect absolutely nothing about you. No IP addresses, no usage logs, no analytics, no tracking cookies. Your session data lives only on your device and is deleted when you close your tab. Read our full Privacy Policy for complete details.
